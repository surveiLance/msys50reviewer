- **Figures:** Rendered all 33 pages. The 6.1 slide 13 network redraw preserves the left-to-right input, three hidden layers, and output, with 5 → 5 → 5 → 5 → 3 nodes and 90 directed connections between adjacent layers. Section 6.2 reproduces the slide 7 fraction, wrong ÷ (wrong + abstained), separate from accuracy over all prompts and the proportion wrong over all prompts. Numerical inputs and policy examples in the interactive lessons are original.
- **6.1 claims with context:** The NorthPole paper reports latency per generated token and energy efficiency in tokens per second per watt; time to first token is a separate measure. Slide 9 qubit counts are estimates. Slide 10 fidelity figures use different experiments; vendor dates are targets. NIST milestones are migration targets and do not predict when a quantum computer can compromise a deployed algorithm.
- **6.2 claims with context:** Size examples are not model categories with hard cutoffs. Slide 7 model names and scores lack an identified protocol. AWS's own accelerator names are Trainium and Inferentia. The robot records are insufficiently specified; the Sony result is task-specific. The MAI-DxO figures depend on the benchmark and setup; the drug is a candidate; the A-Lab authors subsequently revised success counts; the Sakana manuscript met a review threshold and was withdrawn. These numeric and status discrepancies are kept out of the graded bank.
