Scenario clue sheet

Scenario questions hide the concept in a story. Spot the clue, then name the concept.

If the scenario mentions…Think
An outside rule, regulation, or crisis forces actionExternal driver (1.2) · Urgent initiative (2.3)
Top leaders and frontline staff aren't on the same pageVertical alignment (1.2)
Internal processes don't match what customers needHorizontal alignment (1.2)
Strategy can't be supported by the current infrastructureStrategic fit (SAM, 1.2)
The business side and IT side want different thingsFunctional integration (SAM, 1.2)
Whiteboard sketch / formal models / tailored diagrams / version controlIdea / Design / Use / Management (1.1)
Same process at every branch, each keeps its own dataReplication (2.2)
Different businesses share customer data to cross-sellCoordination (2.2)
One central system and one way of working everywhereUnification (2.2)
Unrelated businesses that share nothingDiversification (2.2)
Goal read differently by different people / no IT direction / keeps changingStrategy problem #1 / #2 / #3 (2.2)
Systems or teams left unused after a strategy endsAlignment trap (2.2)
Compare to best practice / certification audit / control objectives / service desk / vendor maturityEFQM / ISO 9001 / COBIT / ITIL / CMMI (2.1)
Shared vocabulary or logging decisions / tailored info per group / more detail for developers / one change breaks other domainsDesign / Communication / Realization / Change (3.1)
Models in different notations (UML vs. BPMN)Integration problem #1 (3.1)
ERD vs. DFD, components vs. functionsIntegration problem #2 (3.1)
What a stakeholder can see / where they standView / Viewpoint (3.2)
Too technical for business; focus on meaningSemantic model (3.2)
